I want to be able click on any text that I see and select "Schedule". My computer should automatically create a reminder or meeting from the text, with no more input from me.

It should figure out a time or date or both, plus a subject, a location, and ideally other people that might be involved. Let me review its guesses before dismissing. This doesn't even need AI.
